Round 3 new model, wiser buyers

Your customers have been through Round 2. They no longer take your word for it. When they price a phone they assume: one year of real support (the warranty), then a phone left on its own, which they value at 40 cents on the dollar.

Design the new phone. As in Round 2, how many you sell each year depends on how long the phone lasts: 100 customers ÷ the years they keep it. A shorter phone means more sales a year, each at a lower price; a longer one, fewer sales at a higher price. Your score is five years of yearly profit, so the replacement cycle is already counted.

You may also announce how many years of updates you promise. Below: what buyers will pay, what it costs to build, and how many you'd sell a year with and without support. Profit isn't shown.
