A classroom game in two rounds

Same keyboard. New letters. How much slower are you?

Type a short paragraph on the QWERTY layout you know. Then type it again after every key has been moved to where it sits on a Dvorak keyboard. The difference in your times is a switching cost, measured in seconds. The class compares times on the scoreboard.

This race needs a physical keyboard. On a phone or tablet the keyboard can't be remapped key by key, so Round 2 won't be a fair test. Use a laptop if you can.
