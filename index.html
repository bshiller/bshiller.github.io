About AI Economics

AI isn't just about what the technology can do; it’s about what people actually do with it. That’s why the best way to predict the future isn't to ask a futurist—it's to ask an economist.

By applying the Incentive Filter, this book strips away the hype to reveal the new "plumbing" of our economy. It translates decades of research into sharp stories about who gets paid and how power shifts when algorithms meet human nature. It’s your guide to spotting the change before it hits your paycheck or your shopping cart.

About Benjamin

Benjamin Shiller is an Associate Professor of Economics at Brandeis University. His research on personalized pricing, ad-blocking, and technological change has been published in leading journals and covered in The Atlantic, The Economist, Forbes, Fortune, The Guardian, and The Washington Post.

With two decades studying AI and disruption, he brings rigorous analysis and accessible storytelling to help readers understand the forces reshaping their world.
