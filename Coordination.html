A live classroom game

Everyone is on Platform A. Platform B is better — if enough of you are on it.

Ten days. Each day, half the class gets a chance to reconsider: stay, switch, or switch back. Your score each day depends on how many of the others are on the same platform as you — and being alone on B pays nothing. Who goes first?
