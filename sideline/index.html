Sideline

Fair playing time for youth soccer coaches.

Sideline tracks who’s on the field, for how long and in which position, and shows each player’s fair share of the minutes as the game goes. It counts only the time each player was actually there, and leaves time in goal out of the split.

It works with no signal, and nothing leaves your phone: there are no accounts, no sign-in and no tracking.

Try it in your browser

iPhone and iPad app: coming soon to the App Store.

What it does

  • A game clock with halves, thirds or quarters, and time added on.
  • A pitch you tap to substitute, plan several changes at once, or switch formation mid-game.
  • Minutes per player and per position, with a fair-share target, updated live.
  • Goals and assists, season totals, and CSV export.
  • Team files you can send to another coach, or keep as a backup.
