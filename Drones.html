A classroom game about arms races

The Drone Dilemma

You run a country. Be the largest one in the class after five rounds. Each round you choose how far to let machines run your war and whether to invade a neighbour, and everyone's choices decide whether there's still a world to win.

Join the game

Pick a country. Everyone starts the same size, whatever the map suggests.

Run a session

Defaults are calibrated for a class of 8–25. Change what you like; students never see this screen. Invasions are always between bordering countries.

Other countries (computer)

Every country on the map plays. Each has a fixed character: how often it invades a neighbour and how quickly it copies a doctrine once it has seen one. None starts an arms race on its own.
