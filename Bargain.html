A classroom game in three rounds, for real money

Your data are for sale. Name your price.

You'll be paired up and handed a private card. Talk for a minute (bluffing allowed), then whoever owns the data makes one take-it-or-leave-it offer on their phone and the other side accepts or refuses on theirs. At the end, one table from one round is drawn at random and paid in cash.
