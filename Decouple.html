  3. Upload the file and reload this page.

The same project you use for the other class games works. Sessions are stored under games/dec-CODE.

Decouple?

The United States and China are deeply economically intertwined. You and a partner will each run one of them for 15 simulated years. Not "decouple or don't" — where, how much, and why — and what that leaves to hold either side back.

Join the class session

This is a model, not a forecast. The numbers illustrate economic mechanisms; economists disagree about their magnitudes.

Run a session

Students open this same page, enter the 4-letter code and their name. When you open Round 1 the game pairs them at random — one runs the United States, one runs China — and an odd student gets a computer partner. Partners should sit together and may talk; moves are still sealed. You open each round; a pair sees its results as soon as both partners submit.
