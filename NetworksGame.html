Racing to the Bottom

This page needs a Firebase Realtime Database so teams can bid from their own devices.

  1. Open this file in a text editor and find FIREBASE_CONFIG near the top of the script.
  2. Paste your project's config from the Firebase console (Project settings → Your apps), including databaseURL.
  3. In Realtime Database → Rules, allow read and write under games/$code.

Everything still works without it — students can play the whole game against a computer rival.
