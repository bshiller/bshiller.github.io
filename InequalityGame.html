What the model assumes

Every response below is a parameter you are invited to argue with. That argument is the point of the exercise.
MechanismAssumption & source
Top earners' response to income taxElasticity of taxable income ≈ 0.25 (Saez, Slemrod & Giertz 2012). Revenue-maximizing top rate lands near the ~73% of Diamond & Saez (2011) once income-shifting is included.
Income shifting into capital gainsWhen the wage rate far exceeds the capital-gains rate and step-up remains open, reported wage income leaks into lightly-taxed gains (Slemrod's "avoidance first" hierarchy).
Superstar inventor migrationDomestic star inventors barely move (elasticity ≈ 0.03); foreign stars are highly mobile (≈ 1.0). Akcigit, Baslandze & Stantcheva (AER 2016). Kicks in above a 70% top rate; slows innovation-driven growth.
Capital-gains realizations (lock-in)Long-run realization response calibrated so revenue peaks near ~30% under current law (CBO/JCT practice; Dowd, McClelland & Muthitacharoen 2015) — and near ~40%+ once step-up is closed (Agersnap & Zidar 2021).
Closing step-up at death≈ $105B over ten years directly (Joint Committee on Taxation), plus the realization-elasticity shift above.
Wealth tax base & avoidance≈ $11T held above $50M; reported wealth falls ≈ 8% per point of tax (between Saez & Zucman 2019 and the larger Swiss responses in Brülhart et al. 2022).
Millionaire migration≈ 2%/yr of the base leaves per point of wealth tax above 0.5% — the France ISF / Norway 2022 dynamic. Departure is much faster than return.
Wealth tax & investor confidenceIntroducing any wealth tax at all cuts investor confidence by ≈ 20%, worsening slightly with the rate — the idea that taxing wealth stocks signals regime risk the way taxing income flows does not. Repealing it restores most — not all — of the lost confidence: markets have learned this government will tax wealth when pressed, and price that in (France's post-2017 experience). This is the least empirically pinned-down mechanism in the model. If you think it's too big (or too small), say what evidence would settle it.
National sales tax revenue≈ $62B per point (CBO Budget Options: a broad 5% VAT-style tax ≈ $310B/yr). Efficient but regressive: it raises the Gini and trims median real income unless paired with transfers (Kleven 2014 on Scandinavia).
"One-time" seizure / expropriationThe taking is announced as a one-off, but expectations are what move markets: investor trust collapses and recovers only slowly even after reversal (Argentina's 2012 YPF seizure and its investment aftermath). The sovereign fund's shares are impaired and underperform under state ownership.
Investment → growthInvestment share of GDP moves with after-tax returns and trust; each point of investment share ≈ 0.1pp of growth. Innovation adds or subtracts up to ~0.4pp.
Deficits, debt & bond marketsInterest rates rise ≈ 2.5bp per point of debt/GDP (Laubach 2009). Sustained deficits >3.5% of GDP beyond baseline trigger a bond-market revolt (UK, Sept 2022); debt past ~120% forces Greek-style austerity. Once the lending ceiling (~150% of GDP) binds, forced cuts carry crisis-sized fiscal multipliers ≈ 1.5 (Blanchard & Leigh 2013) — Greece lost ~25% of GDP closing its gap.
Basic income, $500/mo≈ $1.6T/yr. Poverty falls sharply; aggregate employment effects ≈ 0 at this level (Alaska dividend — Jones & Marinescu 2022).
Basic income, $2,000/mo≈ $6.2T/yr. Work responds at this generosity: the OpenResearch $1,000/mo RCT (Vivalt, Rhodes, Broockman & Miller 2024) found employment −2pp and earnings −12–21¢ per $1 received; SIME/DIME negative-income-tax experiments found hours −7–9% for primary earners. Scaled to $24k/yr: ≈ 3% lower labor input, phased in over five years.
Universal health insurance≈ $1.5T/yr in new federal cost (Urban Institute single-payer estimates). Median families save on premiums; a healthier workforce adds a small productivity gain after a decade.
This is a teaching model: deliberately simple, directionally honest, quantitatively arguable. If you think a parameter is wrong, say so in class — and say what evidence would prove it.
